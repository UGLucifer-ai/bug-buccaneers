Ahoy, matey! I'm Captain Milo of the good ship Golden Gull — the giggliest captain on Gigglewave Bay! Join me crew: hunt the bug, blast the cannon, beat the rival pirates to the Starlight Hoard. Fair winds and sharp eyes — let's go treasure hunting!

⚓ THE CAPTAIN'S ORDERS — HOW TO PLAY

  1. Both pirates open this game on their own phones or tablets. Two screens, one adventure!
  2. On your call, agree your battle plan: enter the same secret crew code, pick the same language — Python, JavaScript, Java, C++, SQL, C, C#, TypeScript, Go, PHP, Ruby or Rust — and the same level, but choose opposite pirate numbers. No peeking at each other's maps!
  3. Then ARRANGE YER FLEET! Tap a ship, tap the sea to hide her, spin her with the big ROTATE button, tap a placed ship to pick her up and move her. Your map is TOP SECRET — it lives only on YOUR phone! Not feeling fancy? Tap Surprise Me! and Captain Milo hides them for you.
  4. Start a voice call with your crew-mate (WhatsApp or a normal phone call) — you'll be shouting treasure coordinates across the waves!
  5. Every round, BOTH screens show the SAME code with one sneaky little bug hiding inside. First to spot it shouts “I SPOTTED THE BUG, CAPTAIN!” and taps the buggy line. Right line = YOU fire the cannons! Wrong line = oops… your rival fires instead. Choose wisely, sharp-eyes!
  6. Shout yer shot! When you fire, shout the square on the call — like “FIRE AT D5!” Yer rival checks THEIR secret map and shouts back what happened: MISS, HIT or SUNK! Then mark it on yer own chart with the big buttons. And when THEY fire at you, tap the square they shouted on YOUR fleet map — your phone reveals the truth for you to shout back. Honest pirates, loud voices, zero peeking!
  1. No crew-mate? No problem! In a Solo Adventure it's just YOU against the computer pirates — no secret code, no phone call needed!
  2. Your rival is the sneaky Captain Barnaclebeard and his Rusty Bucket crew. Pick your language and level below, then tap SET SAIL SOLO!
  3. First, ARRANGE YER FLEET! Tap a ship, tap the sea to hide her, spin her with ROTATE, tap her again to move her — or tap Surprise Me! and let Captain Milo do the hiding. Barnaclebeard hides his own fleet in secret, the sneaky old goat!
  4. Every round, a code snippet hides one sneaky bug — and Barnaclebeard is hunting it too! Watch his sonar sweep fill up: spot the bug and tap the right line before his sonar finishes, and YOU fire the cannons!
  5. Too slow, or a wrong line? Barnaclebeard steals the shot and fires at YOUR fleet all by himself. First to sink the whole rival fleet grabs the Starlight Hoard!
  6. Need a breather mid-battle? Tap ⏸ PAUSE at the top of the battle screen — Barnaclebeard's sonar freezes, his cannons wait, and the music hushes. Nothing moves until you tap RESUME. Take all the time you need, Captain!
